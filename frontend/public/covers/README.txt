Game covers go here.
Name the image after the game's slug (see "slug" in server/g4m3s-manifest.json), e.g.
  drift-hunters-pro.png   friday-night-funkin.jpg   1-on-1-tennis.webp
Supported: png jpg jpeg webp gif svg. Square images (~256-512px) look best.
Games without a cover here show the Liquidform logo.
